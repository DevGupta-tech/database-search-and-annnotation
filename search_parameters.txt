SEARCH PARAMETERS
=================
Database: NCBI non-redundant protein sequences (nr)
Program: BLASTP (protein-protein BLAST)
Query: NP_000509.1, human hemoglobin subunit beta
Query length: 147 amino acids
Maximum target sequences: 100
Expect threshold: 10 (default shown in the archived example)
Word size: 3
Substitution matrix: BLOSUM62
Gap costs: Existence 11; Extension 1
Low-complexity filtering: unchecked in the archived example
Organism filter: none
Entrez query: none
